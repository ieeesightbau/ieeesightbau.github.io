WHAT IS SIGHT?

A global IEEE network, now at BAU Debbieh.

IEEE SIGHT groups are IEEE volunteers who partner with underserved communities and local organizations to use technology for sustainable development. Our group brings that network to Beirut Arab University.

WHAT WE DO

Learn the skills, build the thing, hand it over.

01

Learn

Workshops on the tools behind humanitarian work: solar, water, sensors, low-cost electronics and project design.

02

Build

Small teams turn a real need into a working prototype, from repaired toys to a solar design for a clinic.

03

Serve

We work with local associations, schools and municipalities, and aim to hand over projects people can keep running.
